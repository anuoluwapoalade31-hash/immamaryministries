WHO WE ARE

A mission that proclaims
and demonstrates love.

IMMAMARY MINISTRIES INTERNATIONAL is a Christian, non-governmental organization in Nigeria committed to advancing the Kingdom of God through missions mobilization, leadership development, social welfare and holistic community transformation.

We believe Christian mission involves both proclaiming the Gospel and demonstrating God's love through practical responses to human needs, social inequalities and community vulnerabilities.

FOUNDERS & FAMILY

FOUNDING LEADERSHIP

Emmanuel & Mary
Alade

Founders, IMMAMARY Ministries International

Christian Minister · Missions Leader · Community Transformation Practitioner · Theological Educator

Emmanuel Idowu Alade and Mary Odunola Alade are the founders of IMMAMARY Ministries International. Emmanuel is a Nigerian Christian minister, missions leader, theological educator and community transformation practitioner with over 25 years of experience in Christian ministry, missions mobilization, leadership development, social welfare and community engagement.

His ministry philosophy is rooted in the conviction that Christian mission encompasses both the proclamation of the Gospel and its practical demonstration through compassion, social responsibility, human dignity and holistic community transformation.

Ministry and professional experience

  • Director of Mobilization for West and Central Africa, AIMS Holistic Missions Organization (since 2025).
  • International Trainer in Intercultural Education, Bethany International University, Singapore (since 2018).
  • Former Canon Missioner and Diocesan Director of Missions and Social Welfare in the Anglican Church of Nigeria.

Academic and professional formation

  • Doctor of Ministry in Social Transformation, United Theological Seminary of the Twin Cities, Minnesota, USA (in progress).
  • Master of Arts in Intercultural Education, Bethany International University, Singapore.
  • Master of Divinity in Public Theology/Social Work, Theological College of Northern Nigeria / University of Jos.
  • Master of Theology, Crowther Graduate Theological Seminary / Ajayi Crowther University.
  • Bachelor of Science (Honours) in Physics, University of Ibadan, Nigeria.

He received the 2025 Hawkinson Scholarship Award from United Theological Seminary of the Twin Cities.

Vision for ministry

Emmanuel envisions a Church that is spiritually vibrant, missionally engaged, socially responsible and actively involved in transforming individuals and communities. He is happily married to Mary Odunola Alade, and their marriage is blessed with four children.

“Christian mission must transform lives, strengthen communities and demonstrate the love of Christ in practical ways.”
